WoW Character Suite · Make your own

Make your character page

Your real action bars as a guide anyone can hover: every button, its key and your reason for it. Made from one command in game and two files.

  1. Get the AddOn

    Unzip it into Interface/AddOns inside your game folder, e.g. World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/.

    Download the AddOn

  2. Capture your bars

    In game, type /suite bars. It notes every button and takes a screenshot. Click Reload so WoW saves it.

  3. Drop two files here

    WTF/Account/…/<Realm>/<Character>/SavedVariables/CharacterSuite.lua and the newest picture in Screenshots/.

  4. Add your reasons, download

    Write a note for any button, then download one page to host anywhere: GitHub Pages, Neocities, a Discord upload.

Drop your files here

CharacterSuite.lua, the screenshot, and if you have one, the .character.json you downloaded last time to keep your notes.

Everything happens in this tab. Your files are never uploaded.
