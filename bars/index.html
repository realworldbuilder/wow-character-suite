Rules behind the layout

Keep muscle memory

Leave spells you already press in their slots. Fill new spells into slots you freed up instead of reshuffling the whole bar.

Sort by when, not by spellbook tab

Every pull goes on the number row. Situational goes on Shift. Out of combat goes on a click-only bar.

Group by job

Keep all traps next to each other and all aspects next to each other, so you can find a whole group without looking.

Re-drag after training

A spell already on a bar can stay at its old rank. After a trainer visit, drag it from the spellbook again so the bar has the top rank.

Check movement keys first

Before binding anything, look at Movement Keys. A new binding silently takes the key from whatever had it.

Cut buttons you never press

If you always open with Arcane Shot, Auto Shot doesn't need a slot.
